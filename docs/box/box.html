The grandstands are full, the starter has her flag up and three rivals are already rolling. Race the coast at noon or the bay at dusk, drift through the bends in a real polygon car, and stay ahead of the fuel gauge. Then build your own tracks with the included no-code SDK.
  • Real 3D polygon car, drawn by the console itself
  • Two courses: Seacrest Coast and Sunset Bay
  • Drift with skid marks and tyre smoke
  • Helicopter flyovers and a flag-waving starter
  • Original techno soundtrack and Doppler engines
  • Fuel-gauge racing: checkpoints keep you going
NO-CODE SDK INCLUDEDDraw bends, place palms, oil slicks and grandstands, repaint the sky and reshape your car, then save a new cartridge. No compiler needed.
