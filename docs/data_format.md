# Seacrest Racer: editable ROM data block (format v1)

The track and car editors patch a ROM directly; nothing listed here needs a
recompile. The game reads the block at boot (car) and on track load (tracks).

## Location
- ROM offset `0x030000`, size `0x10000` (64 KB), fixed by `src/md.ld` (`.cdata`).
- The ROM is padded to 256 KB. Editors rewrite the block, then recompute the
  cartridge checksum (sum of 16-bit words from 0x200 to the end, stored at 0x18E).
- All values big-endian. Colours are Genesis CRAM words (`0000 BBB0 GGG0 RRR0`),
  8 levels per channel.
- Default contents are generated by `tools/gen_data.py` during `./build.sh`.

## Header (block offset 0x0000)
| off | type | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0x00 | char[4] | magic `SCRD` |
| 0x04 | u16 | format version (1) |
| 0x06 | u16 | number of tracks (1-4) |
| 0x08 | u16[4] | offset of each track record |
| 0x10 | u16 | offset of the car record |

## Track record (0x2000 bytes each, at 0x0100 / 0x2100 / 0x4100 / 0x6100)
| off | type | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0x000 | char[16] | track name, space padded (A-Z 0-9 space : / . ! - '), shown on the title screen |
| 0x010 | u16 | segments per lap, 32-256 (1 segment = 16 world units) |
| 0x012 | u16 | laps, 1-9 |
| 0x014 | u16 | starting fuel, frames at 60 Hz (game raises anything under 600) |
| 0x016 | u16 | fuel added at the checkpoint |
| 0x018 | u16 | fuel added per lap |
| 0x01A | u16 | checkpoint segment |
| 0x01C | u16[2] | helicopter fly-by segments (0xFFFF = none) |
| 0x020 | u16[16] | ground palette (pal0). Editable: 1-2 sea, 3-4 sand, 5-6 grass, 7-8 road, 9-10 kerb, 11 centre line. 12-15 are HUD colours (locked). Billboards, lamps, cones, grandstands and rocks use this palette. |
| 0x040 | u16[16] | sky palette (pal1). Editable: 1-6 sky bands, 7-8 hills, 9 sun, 10-11 horizon sea, 12 clouds/sails, 13-15 palm colours. Palms and sailboats use this palette. |
| 0x060 | u16 | object count (0-128) |
| 0x064 | obj[128] | 6 bytes each: u16 position (world units), s16 lateral x (8.8 units, negative = left), u8 type, u8 flags (bit0 = mirror). Sorted by position. |
| 0x400 | s16[256] | bend per segment (lateral curvature, 1/65536 per unit²; positive = right). Roughly ±800 is comfortable. |

Object types (art is fixed in the ROM): 0 palm, 1 sailboat, 2 billboard,
3 lamp post, 4 cone, 5 grandstand, 6 rock, 7 flag girl (at most one), 8 oil slick,
9 tyre, 10 shrub, 11 tyre wall, 12 oil drum, 13 beach umbrella.
Oil slicks and tyres are road hazards: crossing an oil slick makes the car
fishtail for about a second, hitting a tyre costs about an eighth of your speed.
Everything else is scenery that cars drive through.

Roadside art is loaded per track into a 276-tile video memory pool (VRAM tiles
1030-1279, 1944-1951 and 2030-2047), first-fit by type, frame by frame. A type
that doesn't fit isn't drawn (and a hazard that isn't drawn has no effect).
Costs in tiles: palm 136, sailboat 27, billboard 63, lamp post 44, cone 10,
grandstand 117, rock 48, oil slick 19, tyre 8, shrub 45, tyre wall 31,
oil drum 11, beach umbrella 57. The game draws at most 30
objects at once (player, rivals and flag girl included).

## Car record (at 0x8100, 0x800 bytes)
| off | type | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0x00 | u16 | vertex count (max 128) |
| 0x02 | u16 | face count (max 96) |
| 0x04 | u16 | top speed, 8.8 units per 60 Hz tick (default 371 = 188 km/h; km/h = value × 128 / 253; clamped 100-600) |
| 0x06 | u16 | acceleration divisor (default 128; higher = slower; min 16) |
| 0x08 | u16 | steering rate (default 30) |
| 0x0A | u16 | drift steering bonus % (default 50) |
| 0x0C | u16 | brake strength per tick (default 5) |
| 0x0E | u16 | grip vs bends % (default 100; min 20) |
| 0x10 | u16[16] | player palette (pal2): 1-4 body shades (also the flag girl's jacket), 5-7 glass (also her jeans), 8 black, 9 grey, 10 tail lights, 11/13 skin, 12 white, 15 hair. The helicopter and the flag girl's outfit share the body colours. |
| 0x30 | u16[16] | rival palette (pal3): 1-4 rival A body, 11-14 rival B body, 5-7 glass, 15 helicopter glass |
| 0x50 | s16[128][3] | vertices, 8.8 units: x right, y up, z forward; car centre on the ground at the origin |
| 0x350 | face[96] | 8 bytes: u8 v[4] (quad, outward winding), u8 material, u8 flags (bit0 = always draw, no back-face cull), u16 reserved |
| 0x700 | char[4] + s16[23] | `PRM1` and the editor's shape-slider values (8.8) in `gen_assets.py` DEFAULT_CAR key order. Editors only; the game ignores it. |

Materials: 0 shadow, 1 body, 2 glass, 3 tyre/black, 4 grey trim, 5 tail light.
Faces draw in record order (painter's order, back to front). The console
computes face normals at boot and rasterises the car at 9 steering poses into a
120×64 pixel box, centring each pose horizontally. Rival cars are pre-drawn
sprites: their colours are editable here, their shape is not.
