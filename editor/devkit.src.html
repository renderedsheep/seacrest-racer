Sky and scenery

The Genesis has 512 colours (8 levels each of red, green and blue), so the picker snaps to those. A/B pairs alternate to make the stripes that sell the speed. Palms and sailboats take their colours from the sky set; billboards, lamps, cones, grandstands and rocks from the ground set.

Art memory

Each kind of object you use on a track loads its pictures into video memory, which has room for 296 tiles (8×8 pixel blocks). Using fewer kinds frees room; the number of copies doesn't matter.

      How this works, and what still needs a rebuild

      The game reads its tracks and its car from a fixed 64 KB data area inside the ROM (at address 0x30000). This page opens a ROM, edits that area, fixes the ROM checksum and hands you a new ROM file. Nothing is recompiled, so the result runs on real hardware through a flash cart as well as in emulators.

      • No rebuild needed: up to four tracks, their bends, length, laps, fuel, checkpoint and helicopter timing, ground and sky colours, roadside object placement, the player car's shape, paint and handling, and the rival colours.
      • Needs a rebuild of the source: new kinds of roadside object (new pictures), the rival car shape (rivals are pre-drawn sprites), more than four tracks, HUD colours and music.
      • The player's body colour is shared with the helicopter and the flag girl's outfit, because they live in the same 16-colour palette.

      • Objects are scenery that cars drive through, except two road hazards: oil slicks make the car fishtail and loose tyres cost speed.
